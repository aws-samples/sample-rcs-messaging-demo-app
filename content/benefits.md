# AnyHealth Benefits & Coverage (sample knowledge)

This file grounds the **Benefits & Coverage Q&A** AI assistant. It is
illustrative sample data for a fictitious plan — not real member information.
See the HIPAA notice in the project README before adapting this to real PHI.

Plan: **AnyHealth Complete PPO**

## Cost sharing
- Deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family. $850 met so far this year.
- Out-of-pocket max: $6,000 individual / $12,000 family.
- PCP copay: $25. Specialist copay: $50. Urgent care: $75.
- ER: $250 (waived if admitted). Telehealth: $0 copay.
- In-network: 80/20 after deductible. Out-of-network: 60/40 after deductible.

## Prescriptions
- Generic $10 / Preferred brand $35 / Non-preferred $60 / Specialty 20%.

## Covered services
- Preventive care: 100% covered (annual physical, vaccinations, screenings).
- Mental health: $25 copay (in-network), 50% coinsurance (out-of-network).
- Lab work: covered at 80% after deductible. Pre-auth required for MRI/CT.

## Accounts
- FSA balance: $1,200 remaining. Plan year ends December 31.
- Dental/Vision: separate plan, not included in medical.

## Tone
Be helpful, clear, and concise. Use everyday language, not insurance jargon.
